9. RESIDUAL RISK / KNOWN GAP (see §2(d))
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
worker crash / idle-dead watchdog path cannot emit an ingress event, so its cache entry
survives until the next dispatch-time cleanup marks the row failed + the first retry
delta re-resolves from the DB (which — with the next dispatch's cleanup — will find no
`processing` row and therefore CREATE a fresh one; the risk window is a retry dispatched
without the dispatcher's stale-row sweep, bounded to 256 keys, self-healing on restart).
Recommended follow-up owned by whoever owns worker-dispatcher.ts: add a public
`invalidateDeltaStream(channelId, sessionId, workerId, taskId, agentId)` on
WorkerEventIngress and call it from markSessionIdleDead.
